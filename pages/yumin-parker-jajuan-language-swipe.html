Lab Charlie: 🧪 What the App Knows

1. Browser Topic: Language

  • What is it? The default language setting of the user's browser or operating system.
  • When would you use it? When loading the main page of a globally accessible, multi-language website.
  • Why would you use it? To enhance the user experience (UX) by automatically displaying the site in their preferred language, saving them the trouble of selecting it manually.
  • How does JS access it? By reading the navigator.language or navigator.languages property.

2. Mobile Topic: Swipe

  • What is it? A mobile touchscreen gesture where a user slides their finger quickly across the screen in a specific direction.
  • When would you use it? To navigate through an image gallery (carousel) or to slide open a hidden side navigation menu.
  • Why would you use it? On a narrow mobile screen, swiping is a much faster, natural, and intuitive interaction compared to tapping tiny buttons.
  • How does JS access it? By capturing the X/Y coordinates when the finger touches the screen (touchstart event) and comparing them to the coordinates when the finger is lifted (touchend event) to calculate the direction.
